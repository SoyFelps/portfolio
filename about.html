    About Me

    I’m Felipe Parreiras, a UX & UI Designer certified by Google. I work as a Product Designer and Project Manager. I create digital products for B2B and B2C and have experience with AI workflows.

    I work across the entire product development process, from discovery, ideation, and research to wireframes, high-fidelity prototypes, and usability testing, directly with clients and Product Owners (POs). My routine includes development plans, sprints, and quality assurance testing.
