    About me

    My name is Felipe Parreiras, and I am a UX & UI Designer / Product Manager. With a solid foundation in UX principles and a strong ability to create intuitive and engaging digital experiences, I am dedicated to developing designs that seamlessly bridge the gap between users and technology.

    As a holder of the Google UX Design Professional Certificate, along with several professional courses, I have developed the skills and knowledge necessary to bring innovative, user-centred designs to life.

    I work across the entire product development process, from discovery, ideation, and research to wireframing, high-fidelity prototyping, and testing. I also work directly with clients and Product Owners (POs). My responsibilities include creating development plans, managing sprints, and conducting quality assurance testing.
