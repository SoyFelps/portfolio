    Design System

    The system behind this portfolio

    Tokens and components used across the site, documented with the real CSS. Everything on this page is live: hover, focus and click it. Click any colour, type style, icon or scale step for details and copy-ready code.

    • Dark theme
    • Montserrat and Roboto
    • Static HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript
    • 0 documented tokens

    Dark first

    One calm dark surface, with panels slightly lighter than the page.

    Accent with intent

    The green is the portfolio’s signature, used for actions, labels and focus.

    Pills act, squares label

    Round shapes are buttons and links. Square shapes are tags.

    Quiet motion

    Short, small movements that confirm an action.

    Foundations

    Colors

    A restrained palette: three surfaces, three text tones, one line colour and a single accent. Click a swatch to copy the value and see its contrast.

    Contrast: text colours pass WCAG AAA on the page background. The line colour is about 1.9:1, so it works for dividers but should never be the only thing that marks a control. Control borders are quiet on purpose and the label carries the meaning.

    Foundations

    Typography

    Montserrat carries all content. Roboto is reserved for interface chrome such as the header. Click a style to see its specification.

    Foundations

    Spacing

    One scale for gaps, padding and rhythm. Click a step to copy its variable.

    Rhythm: sections are separated by --space-24 (96px) and a hairline divider; cards and panels sit --space-5 (20px) apart.

    Foundations

    Radius

    Shape carries meaning: the pill is for anything you press, small squares are for labels, larger radii are for surfaces.

    Foundations

    Borders and focus

    Hairlines separate sections, quiet outlines mark controls, and a clear ring shows where the keyboard is.

    Lines and outlines
    1px solid var(--line)
    Control border1px solid var(--line-strong)
    Accent outline1px solid var(--accent)
    Focus ring: press Tab to move here
    Focusable link2px solid var(--focus), offset 3px

    Foundations

    Elevation

    Surfaces are flat by default. Depth appears on interaction and around devices. Hover the tiles to see it.

    Foundations

    Motion

    Three durations, one easing. Movement is small and always optional: with “reduce motion” on, everything resolves instantly.

    Lift

    Cards and tiles rise 3–4px with a soft shadow. --dur-base (250ms).

    Arrow nudge

    Hover me

    Icons move 2–3px in their own direction. --dur-fast (200ms).

    Reveal

    Content rises 18px while fading in as it enters. --dur-reveal (550ms).

    Foundations

    Layout

    Content sits in a centred column up to 1154px, with generous gutters that tighten on small screens. Resize the window to watch the breakpoint change.

    max 1154px · gutters 62px each side (16px on mobile)
    Wideabove 960px
    Large861–960px
    Medium701–860px
    Small700px and below

    Current width: –

    • Three-column card grids collapse to one column below 960px.
    • Two-column pairs (text and image) stack below 960px, image first.
    • Buttons grow from 44px to 46px tall on small screens for easier tapping.
    • Avoid horizontal scrolling: every component must fit a 375px screen.

    Foundations

    Icons

    Inline SVG on a 24px grid. Interface arrows are 2px strokes that inherit the text colour; glyphs inside cards are filled. Click an icon to copy its markup.
